Free AI plans are good enough for many freelancers. Paid plans make sense when a specific limit is costing you time or money — not because of a feature list.

## Signs the free plan is enough

- You use the tool a few times a week for short tasks.
- You rarely hit usage limits, and when you do, waiting is fine.
- You don't need features such as team sharing or longer inputs.

## Signs an upgrade may pay off

- Usage limits interrupt client work regularly.
- You need to process longer documents or files than the free tier allows.
- The paid plan offers data-handling terms your clients require.

## Do the arithmetic

Estimate the hours per month the paid plan saves, multiply by your effective hourly rate, and compare with the monthly price. If the result isn't clearly positive, stay on the free plan and revisit in a month.

## Try before committing

Use any trial on a real (non-confidential) task, set a reminder before it renews, and cancel if the time savings didn't appear.
