Before uploading a client document to an AI tool, pause and check whether you are allowed to share it, whether the service handles it appropriately and whether the information is necessary for the task. Convenience does not override a contract, confidentiality duty or applicable law.

## Classify the information

Distinguish public information from internal business details, personal data, credentials, financial records and sensitive client material. The more consequential the information, the more careful the workflow should be. If you are unsure what category applies, ask the client or the appropriate privacy contact before uploading.

## Read the relevant service terms

Review the current privacy notice, data retention settings, training-use controls, access management and available business safeguards. Do not assume that settings or protections are identical across free, paid and enterprise plans. Terms can change, so revisit them when your use case or plan changes.

## Minimize what you share

Send only the passages needed for the task. Replace names, email addresses, account numbers and other identifiers with placeholders when they are not essential. Do not upload passwords, API keys or authentication codes. Redaction should remove information from the file itself, not merely cover it visually.

## Respect client agreements

A client contract may restrict subcontractors, cloud processing, data location or use of external tools. If AI processing requires permission, obtain it before use. Keep a record of approved tools and purposes when working with recurring clients.

## Check outputs too

AI-generated text can accidentally reproduce confidential material from the prompt. Before sharing a draft externally, inspect it for private details, incorrect facts and unsupported statements. Keep access to source documents limited and remove temporary files according to your retention policy.

## A pre-upload checklist

- Am I authorized to share this information with this service?
- Can I complete the task with less or anonymized data?
- Have I checked the current plan's data controls and retention terms?
- Are client contracts and legal obligations satisfied?
- Can I review and delete the material when it is no longer needed?

**Bottom line:** Privacy is part of tool selection. If the data permission is unclear, do not upload it until you have clarity.
