An AI tool can generate an answer quickly and still make a workflow slower overall. To decide whether it is worth keeping, measure the complete task: setup, prompting, reviewing, correcting, formatting and any follow-up caused by mistakes.

## Record a baseline

Choose a recurring task and time several normal examples without AI. Record both time and quality. A proposal, report or client email may vary in complexity, so use representative examples rather than the easiest one. Define what “done” means before comparing methods.

## Run a fair trial

Use the AI-assisted workflow on comparable tasks. Include time spent preparing inputs, checking facts and editing the result. Keep the acceptance criteria consistent. If you use different tasks or change the quality standard midway, the comparison becomes less meaningful.

## Track more than minutes

Measure correction rate, factual errors, consistency, client feedback and maintenance effort. A tool that saves ten minutes but creates one serious error may be a poor trade. Some benefits, such as reduced stress or faster exploration, are real but should be described separately from measured time savings.

## Calculate net time

Net time saved equals baseline time minus AI-assisted time, including review and correction. Multiply the result by how often the task occurs in a month. Use a conservative estimate and note uncertainty. Do not convert every saved minute into revenue unless you can realistically use that time productively.

## Decide with a review date

After a short trial, keep the tool if it creates a consistent benefit that justifies cost and risk. Adjust the workflow if quality is good but review takes too long. Cancel or stop using it if results are inconsistent or the task is too sensitive for the available controls.

## A small tracking table

Record task, baseline minutes, assisted minutes, review minutes, corrections, quality notes, tool cost and decision. Three to ten representative examples may reveal more than an informal impression, though the right sample depends on task variability.

**Bottom line:** Measure the whole workflow and quality—not just how fast AI produces a first draft.
